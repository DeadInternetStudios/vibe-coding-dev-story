        Flagged clips

        Save it as flagged-lines.json. Then run npx tsx tools/listen/flags-to-redo.ts flagged-lines.json for the re-render commands.

        Keyboard keys

        They act on the highlighted clip. Nothing plays until you press Space, N, or a Play control.

        Space
        Play or pause
        J Down
        Next clip
        K Up
        Previous clip
        N
        Next clip, and play it
        O
        Mark OK
        F
        Flag. Press 1 to 7 to pick reasons, Enter to save, Esc to cancel
        U
        Remove the mark
        T
        Jump to the next clip still to do
        A
        Auto-advance on or off
        /
        Search
        ?
        This list

        With Auto-advance on, a clip you started is followed by the next one after a short pause, and O or a saved flag moves on and plays the next clip.
