Voice spot check

20 clips, one for each voice, about 2 minutes. Headphones on. Nothing plays until you press play.

Listen for: a word that turns into a note, a joke that lands on the wrong word, a click or a gasp, the wrong kind of voice, a last word that is cut off. The whole pass is listening-pass.html, about an hour.
